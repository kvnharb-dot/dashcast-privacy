01 — Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the DashCast mobile application (the “App”) published on Google Play. DashCast is a weather driving app designed to help drivers anticipate weather and road conditions along their planned route.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By installing or using DashCast, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Public Accessibility: This policy is hosted on a public URL, is accessible worldwide without login, paywall, or authentication, and is linked directly from our Google Play Store listing to meet Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Information We Collect
DashCast is designed with privacy minimization in mind. We do not collect personal account data such as your name, email, phone number, or contacts, and we do not require you to create an account to use the App.

Precise Location
We collect precise location data (GPS) when you use DashCast, with your permission. This is used to determine your current position, your route, and to fetch weather forecasts and driving conditions along that route. Location is collected only while the App is in use unless you grant background location permission for active navigation.

Approximate Location
We may also infer approximate location from network information or when precise location is not available, to provide generalized weather forecasts.

Device Information
We may collect non-personal device information such as device model, operating system version, language settings, and crash logs to ensure compatibility and improve stability. This does not include personal identifiers.

Usage Data
We collect anonymized usage data such as features used, session duration, route query frequency, and performance metrics. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Information We Do NOT Collect
We do not collect personal account data, contact lists, photos, financial information, or advertising identifiers for personalized ads. DashCast does not use social logins.

How We Use Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To provide weather forecasts: Using your location and route to retrieve accurate, hyperlocal weather data and driving condition alerts.
To improve safety: To warn you about severe weather, road hazards, temperature drops, precipitation, and visibility conditions along your journey.
For app functionality: To calculate routes, display maps, estimate travel times with weather overlays, and maintain app performance.
To improve the App: To analyze anonymized usage patterns, fix bugs, and develop new features that enhance driving safety.
For legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms when necessary.
Third-Party Services
To provide weather and mapping features, DashCast shares necessary data with trusted third-party service providers. Data shared is limited to what is required to deliver the service (e.g., latitude/longitude to retrieve weather).

Service
Purpose & Data Shared
Weather Data Providers
Such as OpenWeatherMap, WeatherAPI, or similar. We share approximate or precise location coordinates to obtain forecasts, alerts, and historical weather. See OpenWeatherMap Privacy as an example.
Google Play Services
For location services, push notifications, crash reporting, and app distribution. Google may collect device information per Google Privacy Policy.
Map Services
Such as Google Maps Platform, Mapbox, or similar, if used to display routes and weather overlays. We share location and route data to render maps and calculate directions.
These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of data. We encourage you to review them. We do not authorize these providers to use shared data beyond providing services to DashCast.

No Sale of Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or location data to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not share data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Data is only shared as described in “Third-Party Services” above to operate the App, or when required by law, to protect our rights, or with your explicit consent.

Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

Location data: Processed in real-time to fetch weather and is not stored long-term on our servers. Route data may be cached locally on your device for up to 24 hours to improve performance.
Usage data: Anonymized analytics retained for up to 12 months, then aggregated or deleted.
Device logs: Crash logs retained for up to 90 days.
When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it so it can no longer be linked to you.

Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including:

Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) for all weather API and map requests
Minimization of data collection and no storage of personal identifiers on our servers
Access controls limited to necessary personnel
Regular review of third-party provider security practices
However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices
You have control over your information:

Disable location: You can disable location permissions at any time in Android Settings > Apps > DashCast > Permissions > Location. Note that weather-along-route features will not function without location access.

Uninstall: You can uninstall DashCast at any time. Uninstalling removes locally cached data and stops all data collection.

Request deletion: To request deletion of any analytics data associated with your device, contact us at kevin@dashcastapp.com with your device advertising ID or installation ID found in App Settings.

Opt-out of analytics: If we include optional analytics in the future, you will be able to opt-out in App Settings > Privacy.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights such as access, correction, or objection under GDPR, CCPA, or other laws. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy
DashCast is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is intended for licensed drivers and general audiences.

If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that information promptly. If you believe we may have collected such information, please contact us at kevin@dashcastapp.com.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.

For material changes, we will provide notice within the App or on our Play Store listing prior to the change becoming effective. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of DashCast after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
